House Rules & Equipment Etiquette

We maintain a high standard for our equipment and environment so every player enjoys a professional experience.

  • No Food or Drinks on Table Rails: Drinks must remain on provided high-top pub tables and cue racks. Never place a glass, bottle, or plate on cloth or rails.
  • No Massé or Jump Shots Without Approval: Jump shots and massé shots are strictly prohibited on Snooker and Carom tables. On 9ft tables, jump shots are permitted only with phenolic jump cues and proper slate protection techniques.
  • Cue Care & Rack Etiquette: Racks must be set down gently. Do not bang wooden or aluminum racks onto the cloth. Chalk cues away from the table bed to avoid chalk dust accumulation on the cloth.
  • Respect Surrounding Players: Maintain noise levels during intense tournament play. Avoid standing in another player's line of sight when they are taking a shot on adjacent tables.
  • Zero Tolerance Policy: No gambling disputes, unsportsmanlike behavior, or physical aggression. Violators will be asked to leave immediately without refunds.
