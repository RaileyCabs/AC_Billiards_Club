Club Amenities & On-Site Pro Shop

  • Pro Shop Services & Cue Maintenance

    On-site cue repair master available for fast turnaround tip replacements (Kamui, Tiger, Zan, Predator, Moori), ferrule repairs, shaft cleaning/conditioning, and custom weight adjustments. We stock chalk (Taom V10, Kamui, Master), cue cases, glove accessories, and house cue sales.

  • TouchTunes Digital Jukebox

    Control the room background music directly from your smartphone with the TouchTunes mobile app.

  • Multi-Screen HD Sports Setup

    Never miss a big game or main fight. We stream NFL Sunday Ticket, MLB extra innings, major billiards streams (Matchroom Pool, FargoRate tournaments), and combat sports.

  • Free High-Speed Wi-Fi

    Complimentary high-speed fiber internet access throughout the lounge for streaming, scoring, and working.
