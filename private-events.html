Private Events, Parties & Table Buyouts

Looking for a unique venue to celebrate a milestone birthday, corporate team outing, bachelor/bachelorette party, or private tournament? Atlantic City Billiard Club offers semi-private section rentals, dedicated table reservations, custom catering platters, and full facility buyouts.

Private Event Packages

Package A: Reserved Table Block (8–16 Guests)

Includes:

  • 3 Reserved 9ft Pro Tables or 7ft Valley Tables for 3 hours.
  • Appetizer Sampler Platter (Loaded pub fries, buffalo wings, mozzarella sticks, pretzel sticks).
  • Unlimited fountain soda & dedicated server.
  • Pricing: Starting at $350 (Tax & Gratuity additional).

Package B: VIP Section Rental (15–30 Guests)

Includes:

  • Semi-private lounge area including 5 pro tables for 4 hours.
  • Includes dedicated 12ft Snooker or 3-Cushion table access upon request.
  • Full Kitchen Buffet (Sliders, cheesesteak egg rolls, wings, pizza, fries).
  • Drink tickets or open bar beer/seltzer tab options.
  • Pricing: Starting at $750.

Package C: Full Venue Buyout (Up to 120 Guests)

Includes:

  • Exclusive private access to all 20+ pool tables, Snooker room, Carom table, kitchen, and bar.
  • Custom tournament director to organize a fun mini-tournament for your company or group.
  • Customized catering menu & full bar package options.
  • Perfect for corporate holiday parties, Atlantic City casino trip groups, and large milestone celebrations.
  • Pricing: Custom quote based on date and time.
