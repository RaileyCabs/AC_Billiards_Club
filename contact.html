Location, Directions & Contact

Atlantic City Billiard Club is conveniently situated on Black Horse Pike (US-40 / US-322) in Egg Harbor Township, New Jersey. We are located just minutes from the Atlantic City Expressway, Garden State Parkway, and Atlantic City's famous boardwalk and casino resort district.

Turn-by-Turn Driving Directions

From Atlantic City Boardwalk / Casino District (10–12 Minutes)

  1. Take the Atlantic City Expressway West from downtown Atlantic City.
  2. Take Exit 2 toward US-40 West / Black Horse Pike.
  3. Merge onto Black Horse Pike (US-40 / US-322 West) toward Egg Harbor Township.
  4. Proceed approximately 4 miles. Atlantic City Billiard Club will be on your right in the plaza complex.

From Garden State Parkway (North or South)

  1. Take Garden State Parkway to Exit 37 toward Atlantic City / US-40 / US-322.
  2. Merge onto Black Horse Pike East toward Atlantic City.
  3. Proceed 1.5 miles. Make a U-turn at the traffic light intersection to access the plaza entrance on Black Horse Pike West.

Parking & Accessibility

We offer over 100 well-lit, free parking spaces directly in front of and behind the building. Ground level entry with full ADA wheel-chair accessibility to all tables, kitchen, bar, and restrooms.
